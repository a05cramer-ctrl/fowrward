    How it works

    Three wallets, one clock.

    Seller · the dev

    Lists a share of their coin's creator fees for 7 or 30 days and gets SOL upfront. No waiting on volume, no guessing.

    Buyer

    Pays SOL now, below the expected fees. Receives that share of every creator fee the coin earns until the term ends.

    Forward escrow

    Using pump.fun fee sharing, the sold share is pointed at a Forward escrow wallet. It pays the buyer every hour, then sends the share back to the dev.

    Rules

    Forward's terms.

    1. 01

      Term

      7 days or 30 days. The clock starts when the sold share points at the Forward escrow.

    2. 02

      Price

      Reference price is expected creator fees for the term minus 20% for 7 days or 35% for 30 days. Expected fees = last 24h creator fees × term length.

    3. 03

      Share

      The dev sells a percentage of creator fees through pump.fun fee sharing, which splits fees across up to 10 wallets by percentage.

    4. 04

      Payout

      The escrow pays the buyer every hour, on the hour, for whatever the share earned.

    5. 05

      Return

      When the term ends, the share goes back to the dev automatically. Nothing to claim.

    6. 06

      Risk

      No floor, no cap. If volume dies the buyer earns less; if it runs the buyer keeps the upside.

    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    What if volume dies?

    The buyer takes that risk. That's the trade: the dev swaps uncertain future fees for SOL today, the buyer gets them at a discount and carries the chance that the coin goes quiet.

    And if volume runs?

    The buyer keeps everything the sold share earns until the term ends. The dev already has their SOL.

    Can the dev pull the share mid-term?

    No. The sold share is handed to the Forward escrow through pump.fun fee sharing for the whole term. pump.fun lets a coin change its fee recipients once after launch and then locks the split, so the share can't be pointed elsewhere. When the term ends, the escrow sends that share back to the dev.

    How are fees estimated?

    Trade size × pump.fun's published creator-fee rate: 0.30% on the bonding curve, and on PumpSwap a rate set by market cap, from 0.95% down to 0.05% for the largest coins.

    Are the numbers on this page real?

    Yes. The hourglass and counters read live pump.fun trades through PumpPortal; the pricer reads DexScreener. Fees are estimates at pump.fun's published rates. If a feed drops, the page says so instead of guessing.

    Which coins can sell a forward?

    pump.fun coins that pay a standard creator fee, on the bonding curve or PumpSwap. Cashback and holder-rewards coins route fees differently and aren't priced here.
